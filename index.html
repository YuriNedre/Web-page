FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful information before your mobile detailing appointment.

What do I need to provide at my location?

Nothing. I arrive with professional equipment, cleaning products, my own water supply and my own electricity. You do not need to provide anything.

How long does a detailing appointment take?

It depends on the size of your vehicle and how dirty it is. Most detailing appointments take between one and three hours.

How should I prepare my vehicle?

You may remove personal belongings before the appointment, but it is not required. If personal items remain inside, I will place them carefully in a separate bag and leave the bag in your trunk. I will also remove and dispose of the trash.

Do I need to stay during the detailing?

No. I only need access to the vehicle when I arrive. You do not have to stay at the location. When the detailing is complete, I will send you photos of the finished work and a secure payment link.

Do you offer polishing and ceramic coating?

Yes. I provide paint polishing, paint correction, scratch reduction, wax, butter wax and ceramic coating. Pricing depends on vehicle size and paint condition.

Which areas do you serve?

I serve Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Georgetown, Pflugerville and nearby Central Texas communities.
